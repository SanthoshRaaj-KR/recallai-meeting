# New Hire Orientation

New hires must complete orientation within their first week. Orientation covers company values, organizational structure, and an introduction to the engineering tooling. Each new hire is assigned an onboarding buddy.

# Equipment Provisioning

New employees receive a company laptop on their first day. Hardware requests for additional equipment must be approved by the team manager. Laptops are refreshed every three years.

# Benefits Enrollment

Employees must enroll in benefits within 30 days of their start date. The benefits package includes health, dental, and vision coverage. Open enrollment for changes occurs every November.

# Training Requirements

All new engineers must complete the security awareness training within their first month. Role-specific training is scheduled by the team lead during the first quarter of employment.
