SECTION 1. DATA RETENTION
Application logs are retained for 90 days before automatic deletion. Customer records must be retained for 7 years to satisfy regulatory requirements. Backups are kept for 30 days on a rolling basis.

SECTION 2. ACCESS CONTROL
Employee access to production systems requires approval from a single team manager. Access is reviewed quarterly. Service accounts must use rotating credentials that expire every 90 days.

SECTION 3. INCIDENT RESPONSE
Security incidents must be reported to the security team within 24 hours of discovery. The security team triages all reports within one business day. Confirmed breaches trigger the formal incident response plan.

SECTION 4. PASSWORD POLICY
User passwords must be at least 12 characters long and include a mix of letters, numbers, and symbols. Multi-factor authentication is required for all administrative accounts. Passwords expire every 180 days.
