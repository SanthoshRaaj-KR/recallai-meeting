# Mission and Values

Nimbus Robotics builds collaborative robot arms for small manufacturers. We value safety, transparency, and customer obsession.

# Working Hours

Core collaboration hours are 10 AM to 4 PM in the local time zone. Employees may set their own start and end times outside core hours.

# Meeting Etiquette

Every recurring meeting must have an agenda shared 24 hours in advance. Meetings without an agenda may be declined.

# Communication Channels

Urgent issues go through the on-call pager. Non-urgent discussion happens in team channels, not direct messages.
