SECTION 1. RESPONSE SLA
Support must send a first response to every customer ticket within 24 hours of submission. Tickets are tracked in the support queue.

SECTION 2. ESCALATION PROCEDURE
Unresolved tickets are escalated to tier 2 after 48 hours. Critical outages are escalated to engineering immediately.

SECTION 3. REFUND POLICY
Customers may request a refund within 30 days of purchase. Refunds are processed within five business days of approval.
