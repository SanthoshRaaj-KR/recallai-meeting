NIMBUS ROBOTICS CUSTOMER SUPPORT STANDARD OPERATING PROCEDURE

This document defines how the customer support team receives, handles, escalates, and resolves customer issues. It applies to all support agents, team leads, and on-call engineers who participate in the support process. The goal is consistent, empathetic, and timely service for every Nimbus customer. This SOP is reviewed each quarter and updated as tooling and policies change.

SECTION 1. RESPONSE SLA
Support must send a first response to every customer ticket within 24 hours of submission. Tickets are tracked in the support queue and are never closed without customer confirmation or three documented contact attempts. Priority-one tickets, which involve a production outage for the customer, receive a first response within one hour around the clock. Every response must acknowledge the issue, state the next step, and set an expectation for the following update.

SECTION 2. TICKET PRIORITIZATION
Tickets are triaged into priority one through four based on business impact and urgency. Priority one is a full outage with no workaround, priority two is major degradation, priority three is a minor issue, and priority four is a question or feature request. Priority is reassessed whenever new information arrives. Agents must not downgrade a ticket without explaining the reason in the ticket history.

SECTION 3. ESCALATION PROCEDURE
Unresolved tickets are escalated to tier 2 after 48 hours. Critical outages are escalated to engineering immediately through the on-call pager. Each escalation must include a clear summary, the steps already attempted, and the customer's current impact. Tier 2 acknowledges escalations within two hours during business hours. If a ticket bounces between tiers more than twice, a team lead takes ownership and coordinates a resolution.

SECTION 4. COMMUNICATION STANDARDS
All customer communication is professional, plain-language, and free of internal jargon. Agents provide regular updates even when there is no resolution yet, because silence erodes trust. Sensitive account changes are confirmed through a verified channel before any action is taken. Internal notes are kept separate from customer-visible replies, and agents never share another customer's information.

SECTION 5. REFUND AND CREDIT POLICY
Customers may request a refund within 30 days of purchase. Refunds are processed within five business days of approval, and the customer receives written confirmation. Service credits for SLA breaches are issued automatically based on the contract terms. Refunds above a defined threshold require team lead approval, and all refunds are logged for finance reconciliation. Fraudulent refund requests are escalated to the fraud review queue.

SECTION 6. KNOWLEDGE BASE MAINTENANCE
Every recurring issue should result in a knowledge base article so customers can self-serve. Agents are expected to propose or update at least one article per week based on the tickets they handle. Articles are reviewed for accuracy whenever the underlying product changes. Outdated articles are flagged and corrected promptly to avoid misleading customers.

SECTION 7. AFTER-HOURS AND WEEKEND COVERAGE
A rotating on-call support agent covers nights and weekends for priority-one issues. The on-call agent acknowledges priority-one tickets within thirty minutes and engages engineering on-call when needed. Non-urgent tickets received after hours are handled the next business day. Coverage schedules are published two weeks in advance, and handoffs include a written summary of any open critical issues.
